Signature beverage

The Impound

A bar with no fixed menu. Each day the kitchen and the mandi hand the bar a short list of ingredients at their best (citrus peels, whey, ripe fruit, herb stems, bread, tea leaves) and the bartenders build the day’s drinks from it. No two visits taste the same.

A chalkboard Impound List shows today’s five hero ingredients with weight and source. Alcohol-free drinks use the same ingredients and get equal billing. Indicative prices: bar INR 280–450, zero-proof INR 120–160.

Honesty note: spirits cannot be sourced from surplus. Savings come from mixers, garnishes, infusions and packaging. Licensing and FSSAI requirements will be confirmed before launch.

How the bar works

  • One shared pantry. Peels become cordials, whey becomes a clarified sour, stems become infusions.
  • The Holding Cell. A glass shelf of date-stamped ferments (kanji, shrubs, vinegars) that doubles as décor.
  • Evidence Bottles. Pre-batched cocktails in returnable bottles; return one and earn Tribute credit.
