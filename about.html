About us

The Brand & The Bureau

Story, vision, mission, theme and the people we cook for.

Brand story: why this had to exist

It was a Saturday banquet, 11:40 pm. Guests had gone home happy. In the back, untouched rice, vegetables and bread were being tipped into bins because “that was standard procedure.”

The next morning, at the mandi, perfectly good produce was being marked down because it had missed its slot. Same city. Same system. Two ends of one problem.

The Green Dictate connects them. A Bureau takes the rules seriously so the rest of us can enjoy the meal: it requisitions surplus, inspects it, cooks it and files the results. We do not claim that every ingredient will always be surplus, or that waste can literally become zero. We design purchasing, cooking, preservation, service and recovery so that avoidable waste has fewer places to hide.

That is the new rule.

Brand personality. Playful, formal and benevolent: a mock-authoritarian tone (“Obey the menu”, “Compliance Receipt”, “Amnesty Hour”) that makes responsible behaviour feel like a game, not a lecture. The humour is the doorway; the discipline is real.

Our vision

By 2029, the bin is the most embarrassing room in any restaurant.

Our mission

To prove that a restaurant can be delicious, commercially sound and resource-disciplined by cooking from local, seasonal and surplus ingredients, measuring what it uses, and inviting guests to help write the rules.

Future of Hospitality theme

Group 3 — Sustainable Hospitality

Local · Circular · Resource Conscious. Our assigned theme for the Hospitality Pro League 2026–27.

By 2029, guests will not accept sustainability as a logo, a policy page or a green colour palette. They will want proof. So we built a restaurant where sustainability is the operating system, across the product, the operations and the business model.

What makes this outlet different

The Bureau

A memorable government-office entrance that sets the rules before the meal begins.

The Requisition

Surplus as a controlled sourcing channel, not an afterthought. Source, quantity and acceptance are documented.

The Circular Kitchen

Cooking, preservation and menu design built around ingredient yield and seasonality.

The Countdown Wall

Public metrics connect the guest experience to real operating records.

Rule Four

Guests influence the system, so participation replaces passive consumption.

AI predicts, the kitchen decides

AI forecasts demand and flags anomalies. Food safety, sourcing, menu, pricing and hospitality stay human decisions.

Target customer

The Conscious Explorer

Our core guest is an urban, experience-seeking diner in Delhi NCR who values authenticity and transparency, and who is tired of sustainability claims they cannot verify.

Conscious Explorers

Urban professionals and couples, roughly 25–40, who dine out for experiences and share them.

Food-curious locals

Delhi NCR residents interested in regional ingredients, millets, ferments and seasonal cooking.

Teams, visitors & creators

Companies running team dinners and ESG programmes, plus tourists and creators seeking hidden venues.

Meet Meher

Meher Kapoor, 31, product manager in Gurugram. She eats out two or three times a week and chooses places by story and word of mouth rather than ads. She cares about the environment but distrusts “green” branding. What wins her: the Radius on every dish, the Compliance Receipt, a smaller Ration Size portion, and writing a rule for next month.

Unique selling proposition

Proof is part of the meal

Most sustainable restaurants ask guests to believe them. The Green Dictate lets guests see how the restaurant operates.

The guest journey

  1. DiscoverSee the sustainability promise and the Radius Rule before booking.
  2. Arrive & EnterTake a token, receive a Citizen Card (and optionally submit Tribute: returned bottles, jars or reusables). The notice board opens into The Chamber.
  3. EatA seasonal, low-waste dish with its source and Radius.
  4. ParticipateRefill, learn, shop in The Archive and propose Rule Four.
  5. LeaveTake home a Compliance Receipt: kilos rescued, local spend, food distance and days to 2029.
