The concept

From the street, it looks like a dull government office.

Frosted glass. A token machine. A clerk. A notice board. Take a token, receive your Citizen Card, and the notice board opens into The Chamber: a dim, brass-and-emerald dining room where the menu follows the day’s verified local surplus and the season.

The theatre is the doorway. The real innovation is the kitchen and the operating system behind it. Every dish shows how far its ingredients travelled, resources are measured daily, and AI helps us forecast while people make every decision.

Our story & mission
The four house rules

Sustainability, written as law

RULE 1

Local

Source nearby, employ nearby, tell local stories. Every dish carries a Radius: where it came from and how far it travelled.

RULE 2

Circular

Cook surplus first. Stem-to-root, nose-to-tail, pickled, fermented, dried. Recover what cannot be served.

RULE 3

Resource Conscious

Food waste, water, energy, packaging and occupancy are measured, and the data changes tomorrow’s plan.

RULE 4

Rule Four

Each month one guest-proposed operating rule becomes law. The dictatorship stays participatory.
